Free client-side Base64 encoder and decoder

Use this client-side Base64 tool to convert plain text or files into Base64, or to decode Base64 back to readable text and downloadable files. It supports UTF-8 text, URL-safe Base64, line wrapping, and Base64 data with optional whitespace. Encoding and decoding happen on your device, so your data is not sent to a server.

How to encode or decode Base64

Choose Encode, paste your text, and select Encode to create Base64. Choose Decode, paste Base64 data, and select Decode to restore the original text. For files, use the File Base64 section above. Your data is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded.
